One-line: the process step card — three across, describing how a job runs.

```jsx
<div style={{ display: 'grid', gridTemplateColumns: 'repeat(3, 1fr)', gap: 24 }}>
  <StepCard title="Ring eller skriv">Fortæl os kort om opgaven - vi vender tilbage, oftest samme dag.</StepCard>
</div>
```

- No step numbers: the order is the reading order. Never more than three.
